# Third-Party Asset Notices

This file records attribution and release conditions for third-party art used by Simverse World. The corresponding file-level authorization record is maintained in [`frontend/config/asset-provenance.json`](frontend/config/asset-provenance.json).

## PixyMoon — Cute RPG

Copyright: PixyMoon

Source: [2D Cute RPG Asset Bundle](https://itch.io/s/78711/2d-cute-rpg-asset-bundle)

Simverse World uses nine `CuteRPG_*.png` tilemap files. The public terms permit personal and commercial project use and modification, require credit to PixyMoon, and prohibit resale of the asset pack. The project owner confirmed the intended project distribution on 2026-07-25.

## LimeZu — Modern Interiors

Copyright: LimeZu

Source: [Modern Interiors](https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors)

Simverse World uses `Room_Builder_32x32.png` and five `interiors_pt*.png` files. The paid terms permit commercial and non-commercial project use and editing, require credit to LimeZu, and prohibit standalone resale or redistribution of the source assets. The project owner confirmed the intended project distribution on 2026-07-25. These files must remain part of the Simverse project and must not be offered as a standalone asset download.

## Smallville Collision Helper

Source: [Generative Agents](https://github.com/joonspk-research/generative_agents)

`blocks_1.png` was bundled with the upstream Smallville map assets. Its original pack attribution is not recorded in this repository; the project owner confirmed the rights required for the intended project use and distribution on 2026-07-25.

## Generated Resident Character Assets

The legacy resident textures and portraits were not covered by the 2026-07-25 authorization record, and their SHA-256 values remain permanently denylisted. All 25 `agents/*/texture.png` sprite sheets and 25 `agents/*/portrait.png` portraits were replaced by canonical first-party generation batch `9a11b878c6374ee6b28829e0742b6c06`. Each slot includes a `generation-provenance.json` receipt; both the source and built-`dist` release gates validate those receipts and the generated bytes.
