# Simverse World — Google Flow Production Package

Approved Phase B package for a six-clip, approximately one-minute stick-figure promo.

## Global continuity block

- Output: six approximately 10-second clips, 16:9 landscape, 1280×720 target, 24 FPS, synchronized audio.
- Theme: flat pure-black background with crisp white two-dimensional line art.
- Character lock: every person is the same minimal stick figure with a hollow circular head, completely blank face, no hair, no clothing or accessories, connected anatomy, stable proportions, and uniform medium line weight.
- Accent palette: acid green `#D9FF3F` means city life, awakening, and CTA; coral `#FF4F74` means relationships, personality, and soul; cyan `#63D8FF` means AI, memory, and navigation. No other saturated colors.
- Composition: wide left–center–right staging, lateral camera movement, layered parallax, deliberate negative space, and landscape-safe action.
- Narrator: the identical bright, energetic adult female voice in every clip; natural American English; warm, curious, confident, clear studio sound; approximately 137 words per minute; identical age, timbre, pitch range, accent, microphone character, and loudness.
- Audio arc: one continuous dark cyber-synth identity, beginning with a sparse clock-like pulse, expanding into a rhythmic middle, and resolving in a bright final hit. Voice is always dominant over music and effects.
- Motion language: rapid scene changes, kinetic motion-graphic transformations, and frequent visual events, while preserving an identical stick-figure design, constant line weight, and strict temporal consistency.
- Transition chain: acid-green clock sweep → road; coral dialogue orb → memory portal; cyan memory diamond → Forge core; pixel doorway → city entrance; cyan road ring → portal.
- Text reliability: generate no visible words in Flow. Apply the approved English phrases as clean post-production overlays according to the stitching guide.
- Flow duration adaptation: if the interface only offers an 8-second generation, use Extend to reach at least 10 seconds and trim afterward. Do not speed up, shorten, or rewrite the approved narration.
